## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from the raw, elemental atmosphere of an artisanal Basque *sagardotegi* (ciderhouse) nestled into the foggy cliffs above the Bay of Biscay. The brand voice balances agricultural austerity with visceral warmthâ€”evoking hand-hewn oak barrels, sea mist settling on orchard moss, embers glowing under cast iron grills, and the sharp acidity of wild-fermented Basque cider poured from heights.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Editorial Rustic Minimalism**:
- **Tactile Paper Quality:** Surfaces echo coarse parchment, newsprint cellar menus, and hand-pressed botanical labels.
- **Typographic Rigor:** Evoking traditional European print journalism, vintage wine ledgers, and bespoke bookbinding.
